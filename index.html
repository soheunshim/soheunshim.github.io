About

I am a PhD candidate at the Center for Information and Language Processing (CIS) at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich, supervised by Barbara Plank. I am also affiliated with MCML and MaiNLP.

My research sits at the intersection of speech processing, linguistic diversity, and interpretability. I am particularly interested in how speech and language models represent phonetic, dialectal, and typological variation across the world's languages.
